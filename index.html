Meet Manali Vora

Winner of the National Nutrition Icon Award, Manali is a dietitian and paediatric nutrition expert and one of India's most followed nutritionists. Her career began in sports nutrition, then grew into food entrepreneurship, bringing nutrition-focused food and food budgeting to schools and the hotel industry.

In a world of diet fads and fears, she urges people to use local foods and keep eating simple, blending traditional food wisdom with modern nutritional science for a healthy body and mind.
